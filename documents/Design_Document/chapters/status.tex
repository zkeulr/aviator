\section{Final Status of Requirements}
\begin{enumerate}
      \item Requirement 1: The device must display accurate information.\\
            \textbf{Met}: all information displayed is timestamped and
            discarded once stale. The device is paired with a local
            ADS-B receiver to ensure coverage of local flight since
            open flight APIs lack providers in most areas, ensuring
            overhead flights are accurately captured on-screen.
      \item Requirement 2: The display must not interfere with
            user's well-being by, for example, displaying at
            excessive luminosity, updating rapidly
            in a distracting manner, or being
            excessively bulky. \\
            \textbf{Met}: luminosity is configurable, animations are
            limited to scrolling overflowed text, and the device is
            contained within a 10x5x3 cubic inch volume.
      \item Requirement 3: The device must not infringe on any person's
            reasonable expectation of privacy. \\
            \textbf{Partially met}: the device's location could be
            determined by anyone with access to the API by plotting
            the epicenter of the newly appearing flights, and
            arguably tracking flights violates the privacy of flyers,
            but overall the device leaks very little personally
            identifiable information.
      \item Requirement 4: The device must be language-agnostic wherever possible. \\
            \textbf{Not met}: the interface is
            in English due to the difficulty of displaying
            discernible language-agnostic icons on a screen with
            32x64 resolution. The documentation is also English.
      \item Requirement 5: The device must be responsive and intuitive. \\
            \textbf{Met}: the user interface is limited to swapping
            in between different display modes. All other configuration
            is automatic. The device responds in a fraction of a
            second to user input.
      \item Requirement 6: The device must have robust error handling and recovery. \\
            \textbf{Met}: the device is capable of autonomously recovering
            from any software error.
      \item Requirement 7: The physical device should be easily replicated with
            widely available parts. \\
            \textbf{Met}: by design, every component of the device is
            affordably sourced, with no custom machining needed beyond
            the PCB. A hobbyist or electrical engineering student
            should be able to recreate the device in a few hours.
      \item Requirement 8: The code for the device must be open-source and well-documented. \\
            \textbf{Partially met}: the code and PCB designs are
            published with open-source licenses. However, the
            documentation is unaligned with good practice and lacking in
            several areas.
      \item Requirement 9: The device should be as durable and
            environmentally friendly as possible so as
            not to contribute to e-waste. \\
            \textbf{Partially met}: the device is designed to last a
            minimum of four years of continuous operation, but the
            components are not sourced sustainably, and construction
            is not eco-friendly.
      \item Requirement 10: The device must not contribute to noise
            or visual pollution of any space. \\
            \textbf{Met}: the device emits no sound and displays
            no visually disrupting imagery.
      \item Requirement 11: The device must be energy-efficient. \\
            \textbf{Partially met}: the display consumes approximately 1.3 watts during
            normal operation, 16\% of the maximum possible power consumption of
            the RGB LED display. While not low-power, the device therefore
            does use what power is available conservatively.
      \item Requirement 12: The device must minimize
            construction and recurring costs. \\
            \textbf{Met}: the device costs approximately \$110 USD
            to construct, including the ADS-B SDR. By using only
            free APIs, the device incurs no recurring costs apart
            from energy use.
      \item Requirement 13: The device must not infringe on right to repair. \\
            \textbf{Met}: all information needed to construct, maintain,
            and repair the device is freely available.
      \item Requirement 14: The device must mount and dismount without
            damage to vertical surfaces. \\
            \textbf{Met}: through magnets embedded in the casing, the
            device can attach to magnetic surfaces without adhesive.
            The back of the device is also conducive to non-damaging
            adhesive strips.
\end{enumerate}
