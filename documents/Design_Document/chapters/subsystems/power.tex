% Specifications
\subsubsection{Specifications}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Input Voltage: 5 V supplied through USB-C connector.
    \item Backup Power Source: 3.7 V Li-ion battery (minimum 8000 mAh).
    \item Output Rails:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Regulated 5 V rail for display subsystem (load up to 3 A).
        \item Regulated 3.3 V rail for ESP32 and communications (load up to 1 A).
    \end{itemize}
    \item Voltage Accuracy: ±5\% across all regulated rails.
    \item Average System Power Consumption: < 8 W under typical load.
    \item Conversion Efficiency:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item Buck/Buck-Boost conversion: >= 85\%.
        \item Battery charge/discharge efficiency: >= 80\%.
    \end{itemize}
    \item Protection: Resettable polyfuse (3 A hold), TVS diode for surge protection.
    \item Thermal Requirements: External device surface temperature < 60 °C at full load.
\end{enumerate}

% Interactions
\subsubsection{Subsystem Interactions}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Processing Subsystem}: Supplies a regulated 3.3 V rail to the ESP32, providing stable operation during Wi-Fi transmit peaks and computation bursts.
    \item \textbf{Display Subsystem}: Provides a high-current 5 V rail required for display backlight and brightness spikes.
    \item \textbf{Communications Subsystem}: Shares the same 3.3 V regulated supply for network operations and ADS-B retrieval.
    \item \textbf{PCB Integration}: Works with central board design to route power traces, place bulk and decoupling capacitors, and minimize noise on high-current paths.
\end{itemize}

% Parts
\subsubsection{Parts}
\begin{itemize}
    \item USB-C input controller: STUSB4500 (PD sink, 5 V mode)
    \item Buck-boost regulator: TI TPS63070 (3.3 V / 5 V output from battery or adapter)
    \item Battery charging IC: TP4056 (simple Li-ion charger for 5 V input)
    \item Li-ion battery pack: 3.7 V 8000 mAh flat pack with PCM protection
    \item Fuse: resettable polyfuse (3 A hold)
    \item TVS diode: SMBJ5.0A (for transient suppression)
    \item MOSFET switch: P-channel AO3407A (for reverse protection and load control)
    \item Connectors / Headers: keyed 5 V and 3.3 V output headers with test pads
\end{itemize}

% Algorithm
\subsubsection{Algorithm}
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Initialization: detect input source (USB-C present or battery only).
    \item If USB-C is present: supply system load directly and charge battery at regulated current.
    \item If USB-C is removed: automatically switch to battery through PowerPath MOSFET (ideal-diode behavior).
    \item Monitor: ESP32 reads battery voltage and power-good signal periodically.
    \item Low-voltage event: ESP32 alerts display and reduces brightness to conserve power.
    \item Recharge: when USB-C is reconnected, return to normal operation and log battery status.
\end{enumerate}

% Theory of Operation
\subsubsection{Theory of Operation}
The Power Management Subsystem accepts 5 V from a USB-C adapter as its primary source.  
When external power is present, a charger IC distributes current to both the system load and the Li-ion battery. The buck-boost regulator maintains stable 3.3 V and 5 V rails regardless of whether the system is powered by the adapter or the battery.

If the USB-C connector is unplugged, a PowerPath MOSFET transitions the system to battery power with sub-100 ms interruption. The polyfuse and TVS diode protect against short circuits, surges, and reverse polarity conditions. The ESP32 monitors battery level and manages power-saving behaviors such as brightness reduction.

% Measurements
\subsubsection{Specification Measurements}
[Design Document 3. Every specification above must be validated with measurements.]

\textbf{3.3 V Rail Accuracy (AMS1117-3.3 Test)}

\textbf{Test Setup:}  
The AMS1117-3.3 linear regulator was powered from a 5 V USB supply. Output voltage was measured with a DMM referenced to system ground.

\textbf{Results:}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Input Voltage: 5.0 V  
    \item Target Output: 3.3 V  
    \item Measured Output: 3.3018 V
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Relative Error:}
\[
\text{Error} = 
\frac{3.3018 - 3.30}{3.30} \times 100\% = 0.052\%
\]

This is well within the required ±5\% specification.  
Therefore, the 3.3 V rail accuracy requirement is satisfied.

Additional tests (DD3 requirement):
\begin{itemize}
    \item Battery charge current and termination level.
    \item Buck-boost conversion efficiency under 0.5 A, 1 A, and 2 A loads.
    \item Thermal measurement confirming external temperature < 60 °C at full load.
    \item Failover time from USB → battery measured using oscilloscope (< 100 ms).
\end{itemize}
